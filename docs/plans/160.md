# Plan: #160 make platform-image, reproducible, no EULA acceptance, gaps listed

Objective: a `make platform-image` that, on a Yocto build host, fetches the pins,
runs `bitbake trusted-imx95-image trusted-imx95-image-debug` and writes a SHA-256
manifest of the deploy artefacts outside the clone. It never sets ACCEPT_FSL_EULA
and has no switch that does; when the build stops on a recipe the EULA gates it
exits nonzero, lists the gated recipes and says the owner has not accepted the
EULA. The BSP stays provisional (#14).

## Slices

1. The build script and target.
   - scripts/platform-image-build.sh [DIR [OUT]]: fetch, conf from the templates
     (never overwrites a differing conf), bitbake, SHA256SUMS of the wic image,
     kernel, dtbs and rootfs tarballs in OUT (default DIR/out). It refuses an
     ACCEPT_FSL_EULA in its environment or in a conf, and writes none.
   - Makefile: `platform-image` beside platform-image-parse.
   - test/platform_image_build_test.sh (stub fetcher and bitbake, offline): a clean
     build writes the manifest; an EULA failure exits nonzero and names the
     recipes and the owner's non-acceptance; no generated conf sets
     ACCEPT_FSL_EULA; identical stub outputs give identical manifests; OUT inside
     the clone is refused.
2. Record and gaps.
   - docs/platform-image/build.md: a "make platform-image" section: command,
     outputs, the EULA gap, and the build-twice-and-compare check (written, unrun).
   - This file's gap list below.

## Gaps (each waits on the named owner decision or input)

- EULA-gated recipes (the i.MX firmware and GPU userland blobs): wait on the
  owner accepting or refusing the NXP EULA. Until then `make platform-image`
  is expected to stop on them and list them.
- The real build is unrun: it waits on a Yocto build host with the fetched layers
  and the EULA answer; only the stubbed test has run.
- The reproducibility comparison (two builds, compared) is written, not run: it
  waits on the same build host and on a build that gets past the EULA.
- The BSP baseline is provisional: it waits on the owner's answer on #14.
- No flash: nothing here writes a device; flashing waits on the owner's board
  and key decisions and is out of scope.

## Out of scope

Keys, fuses, life cycle, RPMB, any board step, flashing, AHAB signing, accepting
or scripting the NXP EULA.
