# Plan 175: BL31 from NXP imx-atf at the BSP's revision

Refs #175. Branch `row/175-sw-imx-atf`.

## Objective

Owner ruling 1234 (2026-11-23) counts NXP's `imx-atf` as upstream Arm TF-A. Ruling 771 pinned upstream TF-A `v2.15.0`
and excluded `imx-atf`; upstream lacks i.MX 95 services that `imx-atf` carries (Cortex-M7 SRC, LMM and SCMI base
calls). BL31 is therefore built from `imx-atf` at the commit the pinned BSP release names.

## Slices

1. Pin BL31 to imx-atf.
   - `secure-world/bsp.lock`: `TFA_REPO` is `nxp-imx/imx-atf`, `TFA_COMMIT` is the `SRCREV` of
     `imx-atf_2.14.bb` in the pinned meta-imx commit (`af9eb2c1...`), a commit and not the branch `lf_v2.14`.
   - `test/secure_world_build_test.sh`: the repo check expects imx-atf; a new `branch` case (a branch name in
     `TFA_COMMIT`) must fail the 40-hex check.
   - `docs/secure-world/build.md`: pin row, the TF-A paragraph, the BSP-only services table (provided by the pin vs
     still open), the new build-configuration digest. The old reproducibility hashes belonged to upstream v2.15.0
     and are withdrawn until a host with the toolchain records new ones.
   - The script changes only a comment: it reads the pin by name.
2. The SPEC row states ruling 1234.
   - `docs/SPEC.md` secure-world row and the `ROADMAP.md` secure-world entry state BL31 is NXP imx-atf at the BSP's
     revision (owner ruling 1234) and drop ruling 771's exclusion; ruling 771's other terms stay.

## Out of scope

- The full BL31 and OP-TEE build and the new artefact hashes (no Arm toolchain in the lane; lane-verify or the
  `secure-world-build` CI job).
- Boot of this BL31 on the board; whether the M7 is driven from Linux (tfa-scmi-survey question 3).
- The survey documents, which are history and keep their v2.15.0 text.
